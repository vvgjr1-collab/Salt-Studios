Our focus

Built for how India lives now.

Most Indians moving into cities live in 1–2 BHK apartments, often rented, often shared, and often changed every few years. Furniture made for large Western homes fails them, and cheap flat-pack fails them differently. We design between those two failures.

A

Modular & multifunctional

Pieces that adapt as a household grows.

B

Built for Indian conditions

Knock-down construction, humidity- and dust-ready materials.

C

Made to ship well

Efficient to manufacture, pack, deliver and assemble.

D

Craft at scale

Indian craft clusters bring character to modern homes.
