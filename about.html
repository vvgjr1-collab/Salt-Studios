01 — Our ethos

The most ordinary
ingredient in the kitchen.

Salt never asks for attention, yet without it nothing tastes right. Salt Studios is built on that idea.

We believe the best design isn't the loudest object in the room. It's the one that makes everything around it work better: the table that makes a small living room feel generous, the décor piece that makes a house feel like home, the storage that quietly brings order to a busy household.

Our work is honest in material, fair in price, and considered down to the last joint and hinge. We don't design for the showroom photograph. We design for the tenth year of use.

Rooted in Gujarat

We come from Gujarat, which produces most of India's salt, and from Ahmedabad, where the Dandi March began. That history taught the country that something humble can carry enormous meaning, and that self-reliance starts with making things well at home.

We carry that forward as a studio that designs in India, for Indian homes, with Indian makers.

04 — Mission & vision

Why we exist,
where we're going.

Mission

To design essential furniture and home décor that make compact Indian homes more comfortable, flexible and beautiful, at a price that feels fair and a quality that lasts.

Vision

To become India's most trusted partner to shape better ways of living through design. From the objects we use to the spaces we inhabit, we want to explore what everyday life could look and feel like when design is allowed to evolve with people.

“We don't aim to be the flavour. We aim to bring it out.”

05 — The team

Different roles.
Different perspectives.
One studio.

Salt brings together people at different stages of their design journey. Leadership sets the direction, project leads guide the work, designers shape the ideas, while junior designers and interns bring fresh eyes, questions and new possibilities. Everyone has a role, everyone has responsibility, and everyone has a part to play.

The hierarchy tells you who owns the work. It doesn't tell you who owns the idea.

We believe better work comes from open conversations, regular reviews and the freedom to question. A junior can challenge a senior, an intern can bring a new perspective, and every voice can help the work evolve.

More than colleagues.
One ambition.

We learn from each other, push ideas further and grow through the work we do together.
