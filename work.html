Flagship project

Chauki × Godrej Interio

2025 · Compact living room system

A modular, height-adjustable central table system for nuclear families in urban 2BHK apartments. Tessellating trapezoid modules combine a powered-lift unit, a fixed-height unit and stackable seating pads, so one footprint works as coffee table, dining surface and extra seating.

This is our flagship example of designing for compact Indian living, with manufacturing-ready detail down to the last joint and hinge.
